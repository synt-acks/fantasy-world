## Source elements deliberately not treated as clues

- The repeated name **Philip** is a coincidence and thematic echo, not evidence of bloodline, prophecy, or magical recognition.
- The lightning during the storm is weather, not a manifestation of the wayknife.
- “The End” on the source’s final page closes the draft but does not resolve Father’s condition or the larger character arc.
