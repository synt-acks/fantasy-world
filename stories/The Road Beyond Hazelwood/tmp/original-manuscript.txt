Chapter 1
Every punch hurts worse than the blow before. My name is Philip; I am a shopkeeper’s son in the town of
Hazelwood. All the other kids at school either don’t like me, or like Father says they’re just jealous of me.
Well then why would they chase me every day and beat me up! He just wants me to feel better about it all,
and I don’t blame him either. The school kids, all seven of them, chase me through town, throwing rocks
at me the whole time. They all start to split up, maybe for good today. I look ahead; there are two exits,
the turn to my left, and the turn to my right. I can't go straight because it's the town dock, I’d be trapped.
I turn to my right and the kids that separated are standing there, laughing mockingly. I turn around to go
the left exit, but to no good, I'm already trapped. I run down the dock, hoping something good will
happen. The kids slowly walk towards me, I want to jump in but the current is so powerful it wouldn’t do
me any good. The kids seem to recognize my worry, because they pick me up and toss me into the Big
River, and then they run away, hoping nobody saw what they did. I try to swim but the current is too
strong. I look for a way to climb out, there's nothing. I start to panic, and while I'm wildly thrashing my
arms I brush my hand against some rope. Some rope, I look back and there's rope sitting along the water
up to the edge of the town. I grab hold of the rope and manage to pull myself out, but afterwards I am as
weak as a Forgon. Forgons live alongside the Big River, up near the mountain; they feast on fungus and
smaller creatures. They are really small, about the size of your thumb. The Big River flows from the top of
the mountain that lies to the north; it flows right through Hazelwood and travels south to the great city of
Malcore, where all the merchants and shops are. Every few years Father and I visit Malcore for a few
weeks, gathering the many supplies we will need back at the shop.
I run as fast as I can and make it home about an hour or so later than usual. Father is upset,
“Where were you Philip?”
“They threw me into the river again Father,”
Father stops whatever it was he was doing and stares at me,
“And you didn’t fight back?”
I look to the ground, trying to hide from his disappointment like a mouse from an owl.
“No Father,”
He walks towards me, puts his arms on my shoulders, and quietly but very powerfully says,
“I specifically told you to fight back next time!”
I take a step back, breaking his reach,
“But Father! I can't fight! You know I am not a fighter!”
“Well, you are going to start to become one if these kids keep chasing you. Go into your room and change
into some dry clothes ok?”
“Alright Father,”
I walk up the stairs into the top floor, where my room lays hidden behind a large, wooden door. I step in,
